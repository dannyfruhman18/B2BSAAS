# Client Onboarding Email Sequence (MailerLite)

*5-email sequence. Set up in MailerLite as an automation triggered when a new
contact is added to the "{{ company_name }} Clients" group. Delay between emails
as shown. Sender: your business email. Personalisation tokens are MailerLite
format {$first_name} etc.*

---

## Email 1 — Day 0 (send immediately on signup)
**Subject:** Welcome to Brightwick — here's what happens next

Hi {$first_name},

Great news — your Brightwick setup is underway.

Here's what you can expect over the next 7 days:

✅ Your website is live (or being built)
✅ Your ad copy is ready to upload
✅ Your 30-post social calendar is in your inbox
✅ Your email marketing is set up in MailerLite

I'll be in touch as each piece is delivered. In the meantime, the single most
valuable thing you can do right now is **claim or update your Google Business
Profile** at google.com/business — this is free and dramatically improves how
you appear in local search results.

Any questions, just reply to this email.

Warm regards,
{{ owner_name }}
Brightwick

---

## Email 2 — Day 2 (2 days after signup)
**Subject:** Your social media calendar — what to do this week

Hi {$first_name},

Your 30-post social media calendar is attached (also in the Google Sheet I
shared). Here's how to get started today without it feeling overwhelming:

**This week: just post 3 times.**

Use posts #1, #4, and #5 from the calendar. They're designed to introduce your
business, show your work, and build social proof immediately.

Don't worry about making them perfect — a genuine photo of your work beats a
polished stock image every time.

If you're not sure how to post: Facebook → Create Post, paste the caption from
the calendar, add your photo, and hit Publish. That's it.

Reply to this email if you'd like a quick call to walk through it.

Best,
{{ owner_name }}
Brightwick

---

## Email 3 — Day 5 (5 days after signup)
**Subject:** Quick check-in — how are things going?

Hi {$first_name},

Five days in — how are you finding everything?

A few things worth checking this week:

1. **Google Business Profile** — Have you claimed it? If not, go to
   google.com/business now. Verification takes about 5 days by postcard.
   
2. **Google Ads** — If you'd like to start running your ads, I've set up the
   copy — you just need to create a Google Ads account, paste the copy in, and
   set a daily budget (£5–10 is a good start). I can walk you through it in
   15 minutes if you'd like.
   
3. **Website** — Is there anything you'd like changed? Reply with any edits
   (wrong phone number, a service I missed, anything) and I'll action within 24 hours.

As always, just reply if you have questions.

Best,
{{ owner_name }}
Brightwick

---

## Email 4 — Day 14 (2 weeks after signup)
**Subject:** 2-week update — and a tip to get more reviews

Hi {$first_name},

Two weeks in. How's it going?

**The single fastest thing you can do to boost local search right now:**
Ask your last 5 customers for a Google review.

Here's a template you can copy-paste and send by text:

> "Hi [name], hope you're happy with the work. If you have 2 minutes, a Google
> review would really help the business — here's the link: [your Google Business
> link]. Thank you!"

5 reviews in the next 2 weeks will move your Google Business profile up
significantly. I promise it's worth the awkward ask.

Any questions or anything else you need from me, just reply.

Best,
{{ owner_name }}
Brightwick

---

## Email 5 — Day 30 (1 month after signup)
**Subject:** One month in — let's see how we're doing

Hi {$first_name},

One month since you came on board. Brilliant.

A few things worth reviewing this week:

**Google Analytics / Search Console** (if we've set these up — ask me if not):
- How many people visited your website?
- Which pages did they land on?
- Any search queries you're showing up for?

**Social media:** Which posts got the most engagement? That tells you what to
repeat.

**Ads (if running):** What's your cost-per-click? If it feels high, reply and
we'll look at the targeting together.

**Reviews:** Any new ones? If yes — brilliant. Reply to every single one,
even just "Thank you, really appreciated!"

I'm here for the long haul. If there's anything you'd like to change, add, or
just want to talk through — hit reply.

Here's to month two.

Best,
{{ owner_name }}
Brightwick

---

*In MailerLite: Automations → New Automation → "When subscriber joins group"
→ "{{ company_name }} Clients". Add 5 Email steps with delays of 0/2/5/14/30 days.*
