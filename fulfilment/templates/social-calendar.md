## Notes for the client

1. Post at 7–9am or 5–7pm for best reach on Facebook; 11am or 7pm for Instagram.
2. Reply to every comment within 24 hours — even just a 👍 or "Thanks!".
3. Stories: post daily if possible (behind-scenes snippets, even low-fi ones work).
4. After 30 days, review which posts got the most engagement and repeat that format.
